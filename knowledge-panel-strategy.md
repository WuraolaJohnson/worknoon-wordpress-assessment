## What Is a Google Knowledge Panel?

A **Google Knowledge Panel** is the information box that appears on the right side of Google search results when someone searches for a person, business, or brand. It is populated by Google using structured data, authoritative sources, and entity signals across the web.

For a freelance developer, earning a Knowledge Panel:
- Establishes **professional authority**
- Builds **trust with potential clients**
- Improves **brand visibility** in search
- Creates a **structured digital identity** that Google can confidently display

---

## 🎯 Strategy Overview

The approach is built around **Entity-Based SEO** — making Johnson Wuraola a well-defined, verifiable entity in Google's Knowledge Graph through consistent signals across multiple authoritative platforms.

---

## Phase 1: On-Site Entity Signals

### 1.1 Person Schema Markup (JSON-LD)
Implement a `Person` schema on the homepage and About page that explicitly declares:
- Full legal name
- Job title & professional role
- Website URL
- Profile photo
- `sameAs` links to authoritative external profiles

> See `/schema/person-schema.json` for the implementation.

### 1.2 Consistent NAP (Name, Address, Phone)
Ensure the developer's name, contact email, and website URL appear **identically** across:
- The website footer
- The Contact page
- All external profiles

Inconsistency confuses Google's entity resolution algorithm.

### 1.3 Dedicated About/Bio Page
Create a long-form, first-person bio page (`/about`) that:
- Uses the full name prominently (H1)
- Mentions the profession clearly ("Freelance Web Developer based in Nigeria")
- Links to portfolio projects, certifications, and external profiles
- Uses natural language around known entities (WordPress, Elementor, web development)
